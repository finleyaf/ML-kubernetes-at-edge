Edge Kubernetes clusters are resource-constrained, latency-sensitive, and subject to transient anomalies. This project developed and evaluated a lightweight Kubernetes scheduling pipeline that combines NSA-based anomaly monitoring with short-horizon resource prediction to inform pod placement decisions. In the final online comparison on the homogeneous worker pool, the custom scheduler increased safe placement rate from 64.17\% to 75.83\%, reduced anomalous placements from 35.83\% to 24.17\%, and preserved identical mean scheduling latency while improving startup and total execution time. These results support the claim that a lightweight scheduler informed by anomaly monitoring and prediction can outperform the default Kubernetes scheduler on relevant placement outcomes in a controlled edge-style setting.
